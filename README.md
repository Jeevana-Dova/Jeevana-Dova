### 🔬 Research & Master's Thesis

**Machine Learning for Water Quality Assessment — Passaic River, New Jersey**  
*M.S. in Data Science · Stevens Institute of Technology*

Investigated how streamflow, baseflow conditions, and air temperature relate to E. coli exceedances in the Passaic River at Little Falls, NJ.

- Analyzed environmental data to identify patterns associated with water quality exceedances.
- Developed and compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost classification models.
- Combined statistical analysis and machine learning to support understanding of environmental risk.
